About Charlaine

Making the ordinary, extraordinary.

Charlaine A. Mercado is a Sales Associate with Prominent Properties Sotheby's International Realty at 77 Hudson Street in Jersey City. Her story starts in Jamaica and leads to Jersey City, where she has built a career of more than twenty years.

She began with Liberty Realty in 2004, then Weichert Realtors, followed by three years in acquisitions for the US Masters Residential Property Fund. That investor's eye for value came with her to Century 21, where she earned the Emerald Award, and to Sotheby's International Realty in 2019.

Whether you're buying, selling, renting, investing or relocating, she brings knowledge, professionalism and a great wit to every step.
